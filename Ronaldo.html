THE G.O.A.T

Cristiano Ronaldo is a Portuguese football player. He is the one and only G.O.A.T in football. He played along with many legendary players like Kaka, Sergio Ramos, Luka Modric, Kroos, Benzema in Real Madrid and played with Rooney for Manchester United. He also played for Juventus and recently, for the past 2–3 years, he has been playing for Al-Nassr in the Arab Football League and won the championship. Ronaldo is also a great entrepreneur.
